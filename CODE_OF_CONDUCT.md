# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people,
and do not harass or discriminate. Maintainers may remove comments, commits,
issues and contributions that violate the covenant, and may ban contributors
for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub
(<https://github.com/arafsk>). Reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers who are themselves involved in an incident must
recuse themselves from handling it.
